# ISO 9000

> ISO 9000 describes quality assurance elements in generic terms that can be applied to any business regardless of the products or services offered.

A quality assurance system is defined as the organizational structure, responsibilities, procedures, processes, and resources for implementing quality management. To become registered to ISO 9000, a company's system is audited by third-party auditors for compliance. Successful registration leads to a certificate, with semiannual surveillance audits ensuring continued compliance.
