# Cost Effectiveness of Reviews

> Cost effectiveness of reviews measures the effort saved by finding and fixing errors during reviews instead of later in testing.

The effort to find and fix a requirements error during review is estimated at 6 person-hours, while during testing it is 45 person-hours. Effort saved per error = 45 - 6 = 39 person-hours. For 22 errors found, that's 858 person-hours saved. This leads to shorter delivery cycles and improved time to market.
