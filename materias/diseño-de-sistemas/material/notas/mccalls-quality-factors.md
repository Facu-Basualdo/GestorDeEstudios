# McCall's Quality Factors

> A model proposed by McCall and Walters that organizes software quality factors into three aspects: product operation, product revision, and product transition.

McCall's software quality factors focus on three product aspects:

- **Product Operation**: Correctness, Reliability, Usability, Integrity, Efficiency
- **Product Revision**: Testability, Flexibility, Maintainability
- **Product Transition**: Portability, Reusability, Interoperability

These factors provide a basis for engineering software that provides high levels of user satisfaction by focusing on the overall user experience delivered by the software product. This requires ensuring the requirements specification is correct and defects are removed early in the development process (Max16).
