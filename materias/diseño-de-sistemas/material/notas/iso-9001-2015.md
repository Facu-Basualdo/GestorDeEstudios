# ISO 9001:2015

> ISO 9001:2015 is a standard that specifies requirements for a quality management system, addressing topics such as management responsibility, quality system, contract review, design control, and more.

The standard outlines elements including: establish a quality management system, develop a policy, document the quality system, support quality control, focus on customer satisfaction, define communication mechanisms, establish review mechanisms, identify quality resources, establish control mechanisms for planning, customer requirements, technical activities, project monitoring, define methods for remediation, assess quality data and metrics, and define approach for continuous improvement. For a software organization to become registered, it must establish policies and procedures addressing each requirement and demonstrate they are followed.
