# Review Formality

> Review formality is determined by the degree of planning, defined roles, structured meetings, and follow-up verification in a technical review.

The reference model for technical reviews identifies four characteristics that contribute to formality: (1) distinct roles explicitly defined, (2) sufficient planning and preparation, (3) distinct structure for the review, and (4) follow-up verification. Reviews can range from informal (casual, minimal preparation) to formal (rigorous, with roles and documentation). The level of formality is chosen based on the product, timeline, and people.
